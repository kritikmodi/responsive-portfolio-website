about

Three systems at Datatailr.

I've been at Datatailr since 2023, on the founding team, and I'm the lead AI engineer here. That work is mine to direct: retrieval pipelines, vector search, embeddings, and the MCP servers that put all of it in front of a model. The point is letting someone ask a question of a dataset no model was ever trained on, and get an answer they can put money behind.

The rest of my week splits about evenly between two products. One is the Datatailr platform, which quants and high-frequency traders use to run their strategies without touching what's underneath. Underneath is mine: infrastructure-as-code across AWS, Azure, GCP and Hetzner, private networking on Azure, the DAG scheduler behind every batch job, and the deploy pipelines and monitoring around all of it. Right-sizing that stack took compute spend down about 75%.

The other product I work on is our query engine, a high-speed federation layer that answers across more than 100,000 tables without moving any of them, about 90% faster than the setup it replaced.

Before that I wrote Rust at Juspay on Hyperswitch, their open-source payment orchestrator. Before that, internships at IBM and Brane, and a GitHub Externship with Suborbital where I spent a few months writing WebAssembly runnables.

about

Query engines, infra, and a lot of AI.

I've been at Datatailr since 2023, on the founding team. The product is a computing platform for finance. My main piece of it is the query engine, a high-speed federation engine that answers across more than 100,000 tables without moving any of them.

Around that I do the infrastructure and DevOps: infrastructure-as-code across four clouds, the private networking on Azure, the DAG scheduler that runs batch work, and the deploy pipelines and monitoring around all of it.

A good chunk of my week is AI work, and it keeps growing. Retrieval pipelines, vector search, embeddings and MCP servers, mostly so people can ask questions of data the models have never seen.

Before that I wrote Rust at Juspay on Hyperswitch, their open-source payment orchestrator. Before that, internships at IBM and Brane, and a GitHub Externship with Suborbital where I spent a few months writing WebAssembly runnables.

I didn't start in engineering. Most of my first paid work was posters, logos and UI for small companies, and I still take the visual side of a project when it needs one. I've also been doing competitive programming since school, which is where most of my problem-solving habits come from.

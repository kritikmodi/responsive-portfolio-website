about

Datatailr, since 2023.

I joined Datatailr in 2023 on the founding team and I'm the lead AI engineer now. I run the retrieval work. That means pipelines, vector search, embeddings, and MCP servers that expose them to a model.

The rest of my week splits between two products. One is the Datatailr platform, where quants and high-frequency traders run their strategies. I handle everything under it: infrastructure-as-code across AWS, Azure, GCP and Hetzner, private networking on Azure, the DAG scheduler behind every batch job, deploy pipelines, monitoring. Right-sizing that stack cut the compute bill by three quarters.

The other is the query engine. It answers across more than 100,000 tables without copying any of them, and it runs roughly ten times faster than what it replaced.

Before Datatailr I wrote Rust at Juspay on Hyperswitch, their open-source payment orchestrator. Earlier than that, internships at IBM and Brane, and a GitHub Externship with Suborbital writing WebAssembly runnables.
